About Us

Chromelite Technologies Limited is a Kampala-based company specializing in branding, signage and structural steel supply. We are committed to delivering professional, innovative and reliable solutions that help businesses establish strong visual identities and achieve their construction and branding objectives.

Our company focuses on quality, creativity, customer satisfaction and timely service delivery. We work with individuals, businesses and organizations to provide customized solutions that meet their specific needs while maintaining high professional standards.

Our Services

We offer a range of professional services designed to support business branding, advertising, printing and construction requirements.

Our experienced team is dedicated to providing quality products and dependable services for projects of different sizes.

Signage Solutions

Design, production and installation of professional indoor and outdoor signs, illuminated signs and business displays.

Vehicle Branding

Professional vehicle graphics, car wraps and fleet branding to help businesses promote their brands on the road.

Printing Services

Quality printing solutions for banners, posters, brochures, business cards and other promotional materials.

Steel Fabrication

Supply and fabrication of structural steel products for construction, industrial and commercial applications.

Our Projects

Chromelite Technologies Limited undertakes branding, signage and steel-related projects aimed at helping clients improve their business visibility and infrastructure.

Our project portfolio includes signage installations, vehicle branding, promotional printing and structural steel supply. We emphasize attention to detail, quality materials and professional project execution.
